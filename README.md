# Depths of Karath

A mobile-first roguelike dungeon crawler. Descend 10 floors, defeat Karath the Undying.

## Classes
- **Warrior** – 85 HP, 10 ATK, 5 DEF. Skill: Shieldwall (CD 4)
- **Rogue** – 55 HP, 13 ATK, 2 DEF. Skill: Smoke Bomb (CD 3)
- **Mage** – 45 HP, 8 ATK, 2 DEF. Skill: Arcane Burst (CD 5)

## Controls
**Dungeon:** D-pad to move. Bump into enemies to attack. Tap BAG for inventory.  
**Combat:** ATTACK, SKILL, ITEM, FLEE buttons.  
**Keyboard:** WASD / Arrow keys to move. Z = Skill, X = Bag, Space = Attack.

## Floors
- 10 procedurally generated floors
- Boss on Floor 5: Bone Colossus
- Final Boss on Floor 10: Karath the Undying (has Phase 2!)
